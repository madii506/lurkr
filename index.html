05questions

Straight answers.

What is a lurkr?

A small web crawler you own. It starts at one page, reads it, collects its links and reads the most promising ones, one at a time. Every page that mentions one of your words is saved with the passage around the match.

Does it keep going when I close the tab?

Not yet. Today your lurkrs run while this page is open, and everything they find is saved in this browser. Lurking while you sleep needs a small server-side store; it is the next thing we switch on.

Is it allowed to read any site?

Only public pages, and only where the site's robots.txt allows crawlers. It identifies itself as lurkrbot, waits between visits to the same site, never logs in and never fills in forms.

Are the finds true?

A find means the page mentions your word. It is matched, not checked: read the passage and open the page before you act on anything.

Why did it get stuck?

Its start page wouldn't load: the site blocked crawlers, the address was wrong, or the page isn't HTML. The status tells you why, and it tries again by itself.

What does it learn?

Which kinds of links lead to finds. Each link it follows is judged, and the words in that link gain or lose weight. Its card shows what it heads for, what it avoids, and how its hit rate changed.
